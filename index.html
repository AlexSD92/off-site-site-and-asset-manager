Quick tips

Drag to draw lots, independent area boxes or walls. Boxes have custom labels and colours without invoices. Tap a lot with Green Off to clear it. Select anything to edit its details.

Use Delete Item to tap an object to remove it. Undo restores deleted items. Mouse wheel to zoom, or drag empty space while using Select to pan without moving objects. Space-drag and middle-drag also pan. Use Move All to move the drawing itself. On mobile, use Pan or pinch.
